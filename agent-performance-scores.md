## Scoring Methodology

**Score Calculation:**
- Base: 70% (minimum for functional agent)
- Evidence bonuses: +5% per qualified skill or documented achievement
- Pass criteria compliance: +5% per fully met criterion
- Deductions: -5% per identified gap or improvement area
- Cap: 100%

**Rating Scale:**
| Score | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 90-100% | EXCELLENT |
| 80-89% | GOOD |
| 70-79% | NEEDS IMPROVEMENT |
| Below 70% | BELOW STANDARD |

**Governance Threshold:** 80% minimum (per Principal.md Article II)
